AI Overview / GEO Target Snippet Executive Summary

IS4340: Search Engine Optimization & Generative Engine Optimization is an advanced university course designed for business and information systems students. It bridges classic organic search retrieval algorithms with emerging generative AI search platforms. Students learn to optimize digital assets for dual visibility: ranking in traditional search engines (Google, Baidu, Bing) and being cited as authoritative sources in Generative AI Answer Engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Google AI Overviews).

Traditional SEO

A marketing and technical discipline focused on improving visibility in non-paid (organic) search engine result pages. Optimizes HTML markup, keyword intent, page load speed, and inbound backlink authority to maximize click-through traffic (CTR).

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

The next-generation optimization paradigm tailored for Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engines. Optimizes semantic entity clarity, factual source credibility, Schema JSON-LD data, and authoritative quotes to ensure content is retrieved and cited in direct AI synthesized answers.

Search Engine Basics: Algorithm Ranking & AI Mechanics

Deconstructs the algorithmic machinery powering both legacy search and generative AI. Compares traditional web crawling and inverted index retrieval with modern Large Language Models (LLMs), Vector Embeddings, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Evaluates positive ranking signals, negative penalties, and regional engines.

Core Learning Objectives & Key Concepts

  • Traditional algorithmic ranking phases: High-authority seed crawling, inverted index construction, and multi-factor ranking.
  • The mechanics of AI Search: LLMs (e.g. GPT-4/GPT-5, Gemini) acting as reasoning synthesis engines paired with real-time RAG retrieval.
  • Vector Search & Semantic Proximity: Ranking documents by conceptual meaning in high-dimensional embedding spaces rather than exact keyword string matching.
  • Crawler visibility boundaries: The primacy of semantic HTML text vs client-side JavaScript, canvas, and deprecated plugins.
  • Ranking factor analysis: Domain trustworthiness and E-E-A-T vs severe negative penalties (malware hosting, cloaking, deceptive doorway redirects).
  • Global search landscape: China's Baidu (~70% market share), Russia's Yandex, South Korea's Naver, and standalone AI engines like Perplexity.
